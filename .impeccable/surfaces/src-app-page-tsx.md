## Scope

All screens of the web app: library, song practice view, setlists, and the teacher's editor. Visitor mode: Operate.

## Audience and task

Beginner worship guitarists practising with a guitar in hand (phone on a stand, or laptop); the teacher charting songs on a laptop. Main task: follow the chords and strum pattern in time with the YouTube video, in easy or advanced mode.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The song is read the way light comes through a church window: sections are panes of colour and the beat moves through them. Refuses the category default of a grey lyric sheet with chords floating over words.

OWN-WORLD: Stained glass held to an app's four allowances. Ground is near-black "lead" (#121212 family); surfaces one tone step up. Four jewel hues, each with one job: ruby for chorus and kick, cobalt for verse and snare, amber for the current beat and hi-hat, green for bridge. One grotesk with tabular figures. Panes have small radius and dark gaps between them, never outlines.

STORY: The student sees which section they are in, the chord now and next at arm's length, and the strum grid lighting up step by step; they loop the hard part and slow it down.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Practice view. Video at left (desktop) or top (phone), large now/next chord with its diagram beside it, the strum grid under it. Below, the chart as rows of bar panes coloured by section. Mode, key/capo, speed and loop sit in one control bar under the video.

FORM: Candidate 6 of 7 (stained glass), assigned by the roll. seed:329ae0ee0013296d

SIGNATURE: The strum grid as leaded panes: kick, snare and hi-hat rows are panes tinted ruby, cobalt and amber by strength; the stroke row shows arrows; the step playing now glows like sun through glass.
